# AI-Powered Retail Intelligence & Sales Forecasting Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end retail analytics solution designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and business trends using data analytics and machine learning techniques.

The project combines Python, MySQL, Power BI, RFM Customer Segmentation, K-Means Clustering, and Sales Forecasting to generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products
- Analyze category, sub-category, region, market, and customer performance
- Perform customer segmentation using RFM analysis
- Apply K-Means clustering to identify customer groups
- Forecast future sales using machine learning
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis was used to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior.

Customer segments include:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- At Risk
- Others

K-Means clustering was also applied to identify customer groups based on RFM characteristics.

## 📈 Sales Forecasting

A machine learning model was developed to forecast future monthly sales using historical sales patterns and time-based features.

The forecasting process includes:

- Monthly sales aggregation
- Time-based feature engineering
- Random Forest Regression
- Future sales prediction
- Actual vs Forecast visualization

## 💡 Business Insights

### Sales Performance

Technology is the highest-performing category by sales, indicating strong customer demand for technology products.

### Customer Insights

A small group of top customers contributes significantly to overall sales, highlighting the importance of retaining high-value customers.

### Product & Profitability

Product performance varies across sub-categories, with some products generating strong sales while others contribute lower profitability.

### Strategic Recommendations

Sales trends, customer segments, product profitability, and discount patterns can be used to support data-driven business decisions.
